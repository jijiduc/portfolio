# Argue opinions and choices in decision-making processes

Rely on theoretical justifications, logical demonstrations, and quantitative or qualitative evaluations, and debate fairly and respectfully the arguments of other parties.

---

??? example "W1 · TEXTurePaper solution assessment"
    **Action:** Argued why TEXTurePaper was a promising but ultimately unsuitable solution.

    **Justification:** The argument was structured around findings — output format, dependency stability, model availability, implementation opacity. 

    **Reference:** [Week 1](../../journal/week1.md)

??? example "W2 · Horizontal leadership critique"
    **Action:** Argued against horizontal leadership in an 8-person team context.

    **Justification:** The argument was based on experience of the observed consequences (unprepared client meeting, duplicated work, no accountability). I proposed a concrete alternative (team leader + sub-team leads) with justification.

    **Reference:** [Week 2](../../journal/week2.md)
