# Critically reflect on a production process

Step back to describe one's reflection and decision paths, judge a posteriori the correctness and adequacy of what was produced, and suggest reasoned and motivated alternatives.

---

??? example "W1 · Self-reflection"
    **What went well:**

    - Found a promising solution for the texture projection problem.
    - Team atmosphere was productive and goal-oriented.

    **What could be improved:**

    - Meeting time was not always efficient — should have been more proactive in keeping them short.
    - Reflections on problems should be as granular as possible to ensure complete coverage.
    - Should remain alert to edge cases when scoping a task.

    **What was learned:**

    - Always source displayed information with references.
    - "What we will or won't produce" is a client decision, not a team decision.
    - When testing a solution, push 5–10 cases to quantify complexity properly.
    - A promising solution may no longer be viable — verify currency before committing.

    **Reference:** [Week 1](../../journal/week1.md)

??? example "W2 · Self-reflection"
    **What went well:**

    - The tracing team successfully implemented the planned pipeline.
    - Daily meetings were shorter and more focused than Week 1.

    **What could be improved:**

    - Client meeting preparation — lack of assigned ownership meant nobody prepared.
    - Communication gaps led to some work being wasted. A team lead with an overview would prevent this.

    **What was learned:**

    - The client has limited time and ability to parse technical language — always vulgarize.
    - Accountability is a motivator that must be explicitly assigned, not assumed.
    - Testing multiple solutions requires a well-defined, automatable test pipeline.
    - A person with a global overview of the project is necessary to avoid blind duplication of work.

    **Reference:** [Week 2](../../journal/week2.md)

??? example "W3 · Self-reflection"
    **What went well:**

    - The project is on track toward its objectives.

    **What could be improved:**

    - Client meeting preparation still insufficient — need to bring decision-enabling data.
    - Working atmosphere sometimes unproductive — isolation and focus should be prioritised.
    - Personal implementation speed needs improvement.

    **What was learned:**

    - When a team cannot make a decision, a superior must step in based on the best available evidence.
    - There is no shame in asking for help.
    - In a team, a single failure is a team failure.

    **Reference:** [Week 3](../../journal/week3.md)
