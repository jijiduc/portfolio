**Q3: In a team effort involving 8 personn like this project, is an horizontal leadership a good way to lead the team toward completion ?**

**A:** No, in an horizontal leadership environement, simultaneously everyone and nobody is responsible for anything. This will certainly lead to unprepared situations. For example, if nobody is clearly responsible toward success, then the urge to work could be decreased.
A system with a group leader and specific dev's team leader will ensure more accountability to the work and ensure a better flow.
